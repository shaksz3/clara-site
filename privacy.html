Privacy Policy

Clara runs on your own computer. There is no Clara account and no Clara server that receives your data.

What Clara stores, and where

Your recordings, clips, captions, settings and the sign-in tokens for accounts you connect are stored on your PC only.

What Clara sends, and to whom

When you post or send a draft, Clara sends that video and the words you wrote for it directly to the service you chose (for example TikTok), using the access you granted. If you turn on the optional writing assistant, the words spoken in a clip are sent to that assistant's provider to suggest headlines. Clara does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

TikTok data

When you connect TikTok, Clara reads your basic profile (name and picture) to show which account is connected, and uploads the videos you choose as drafts. It does not read your other TikTok content.

Removing your data

Disconnect an account in Clara to delete its sign-in token from your PC. Deleting Clara's data folder removes everything Clara stored. You can also revoke Clara's access in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions.
