Clara

Clara is a desktop app for Windows that turns your livestream recordings into short, captioned vertical clips and helps you post them to your own accounts.

Clara runs on your own PC. Your recordings, clips and sign-ins stay on your computer.

Posting to TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, Clara can send a finished clip to that account as a draft. You review it in the TikTok app and decide whether and how to post it.
